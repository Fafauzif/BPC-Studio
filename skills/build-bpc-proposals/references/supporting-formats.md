# Supporting formats

Use this module only for requested BMCs, pitch decks, scripts or judge defense. The complete written proposal is the primary product. Do not automatically generate every format, or let a compressed format substitute for missing commercial reasoning.

## Establish the canonical business

Recover the latest accepted proposal, venture definition, customer, earning unit, prices, volumes, operating roles and evidence. If a supporting format reveals a contradiction, repair the canonical model and propagate the change to affected artifacts. Preserve protected decisions; do not silently invent traction to make a slide persuasive.

Identify the audience, purpose and actual page/time rules. Default language remains Indonesian unless requested otherwise. A short delivery can still describe an operating business confidently without pretending it has achieved sales, approvals or partnerships.

## BMC: compress relationships

Build native editable text and cells when the requested destination supports them. Use short concrete entries, with functions explained before internal brand labels. The nine blocks should fit together: segments receive a specific value, channels produce an accessible purchase route, relationships support acquisition/repeat use, resources/activities/partners deliver it, and costs/revenues reflect the same transactions.

Audit these links:

| Link | Question |
|---|---|
| Segment → value | Which buyer need changes, and who pays? |
| Channel → relationship | How does discovery become an order and repeat purchase? |
| Activity → resource/partner | Who actually fulfills and controls quality? |
| Revenue → cost | What chargeable unit earns money, and what does it consume? |

Investor capital belongs to financing, not customer revenue. Channels selling the same SKU are not automatically different revenue models. A potential partner category does not imply a signed named agreement. Put decisive detail in the supporting explanation or proposal; tiny unreadable cells do not create completeness.

## Pitch deck and speaking script

Select a causal sequence that fits the actual time: customer decision and friction, complete offer, why it is chosen, paid transaction, reachable acquisition, fulfillment, economics, expansion and requested support. Combine or omit material based on relevance and rules. An executive deck should make the business understandable without reading the proposal.

Give each slide a message, an exhibit or example that supports it, and a transition to the next decision. State units, periods and assumptions near financial figures. Use precise editable charts for exact data; label conceptual visuals and mockups according to what they show. A product screen is not evidence of active users.

Write the script for speech with natural Indonesian sentences, deliberate emphasis and transitions. Distinguish what appears on the slide from what the speaker explains. Time the actual script at an appropriate speaking pace and leave space for transitions or demonstrations. Do not claim a timed rehearsal unless it occurred. Use the presentation production workflow for PPTX creation and rendering.

## Judge defense and Q&A

Build a question bank from weaknesses that could change the recommendation: buyer choice, strongest substitute, revenue recognition, attainable volume, capacity, cash timing, quality, evidence and expansion. Answer directly, then give the relevant reasoning/source and implication. Keep answers consistent with the canonical numbers.

For an unsupported achievement, correct the premise: describe the adopted design or conditional forecast and the specific evidence actually available. Do not hide a material dependency, manufacture a test, or end every answer with a vague promise of further validation. Acknowledge an uncertainty once, explain its business consequence and the chosen response.

## Delivery checks

Check cross-format names, prices, units, dates, claims and totals. Review readability in the actual delivered pages/slides; verify that compressed tables and diagrams remain legible. Deliver only the authorized formats and concise use notes. Local preparation does not authorize external publication, submission, partner contact or personal-skill installation.
