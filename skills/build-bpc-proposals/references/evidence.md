# Evidence and research

Use this module when finding sources, sizing markets, supporting a claim, interpreting supplied research or reviewing factual reliability. Evidence should answer a business decision. A large bibliography cannot repair an unclear customer, unsupported sales volume or contradictory operating model.

## Read the material that is actually supplied

Read current task documents through their available content. Extract full text as preparation, then inspect substantive tables, graphs, diagrams and images from the source pages. A file inventory, search result, abstract or OCR run is not a completed reading claim. Record physical page numbers for unreadable content, missing appendices, broken links and external supplements not opened. Distinguish a truly blank page from extraction failure.

Use selected corpus lessons for craft; reopen an original when its passage, calculation or figure matters. Do not describe an example as a winner without evidence. Repeated language across examples is not independent proof. Their identities, surveys, financial inputs and achievements remain confined to their cases.

## Match the evidence to the claim

Maintain a working record with claim, intended use, source/locator, date, population/unit, method, limitation and body location. Track evidence state separately from the decision to adopt a design.

| Claim | Useful basis | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase behavior | Observed transactions and their context | Does not automatically generalize to a new segment |
| Customer decision | Interviews with recruitment and question context | Explains reasoning, not population prevalence |
| Survey percentage | Sample, recruitment, wording and denominator | Describes that recruited sample |
| Technical performance | Relevant test method, sample and conditions | Material research is not automatically product performance |
| Future revenue | Explicit operating and acquisition drivers | Conditional projection, not achieved sales |

Distinguish external research, proposed tests, tests actually performed and measured results. A mockup is evidence of a design artifact, not a working system or user outcome. A named potential partner is not a signed agreement.

## Research in the order of decisions

Define the fact that could change the business case. Prefer primary, authoritative and appropriately current sources; use official competition rules for compliance and current governing sources when legal or regulatory accuracy matters. Consult relevant connected sources when available. Read the original supporting material rather than copying search snippets.

Check whether geography, period, customer unit and definition match. Trace citations to the precise proposition they support. A citation about an ingredient cannot establish safety or efficacy of the assembled product. A non-significant statistical result does not by itself establish equivalence. Resolve conflicting sources by their methods and relevance; preserve material uncertainty.

## Build reachable market logic

Separate total opportunity, eligible/serviceable scope and sales reachable through actual channels and capacity. Retain unit, period, geography, exclusions, overlap and price basis. Count buyers, users, orders and physical units separately.

A practical route is: reachable accounts or qualified traffic → conversion assumption → orders → repeat frequency → units/revenue, constrained by fulfillment. Compare this with an independent contextual estimate when useful. Do not select an arbitrary fraction of a giant population and label it attainable sales.

## Integrate evidence without weakening the prose

Place material assumptions beside the result they govern. Write adopted operations directly and introduce forecasts as projections. Keep detailed evidence labels backstage; repeat a boundary when a detached figure would otherwise mislead.

When support is missing, verify, narrow the claim, explain the conditional result, or redesign the relevant element. Do not fabricate interviews, sample sizes, test results, licenses or funding. Deliver a connected argument with traceable citations and inspectable appendices, plus precise remaining gaps in the project record.
