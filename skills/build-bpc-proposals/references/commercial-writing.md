# Commercial writing

The [manual](bpc-manual.md) governs scope and truthfulness. Sell the complete business: why a customer chooses the offer, what is bought, how value becomes revenue, who delivers it, why customers return and how the configuration grows. Confidence comes from these relationships, not inflated adjectives or unsupported traction.

## Commercial paragraphs

Give each paragraph a relationship to develop. Customer friction motivates the offer; the offer explains a purchase; channels produce demand; demand creates paid work; work determines costs; cash and quality determine expansion. Use connected prose and natural transitions. Tables preserve exact comparable records rather than replacing the argument.

Weak: “Usaha ini merupakan inovasi revolusioner yang mengintegrasikan digitalisasi, sustainability, dan pemberdayaan masyarakat.”

Stronger: “Pelanggan memilih ukuran rak berdasarkan barang yang ingin disimpan. Paket mencakup pemasangan, sehingga pelanggan mengetahui biaya ruang yang selesai dipakai sebelum memesan. Perusahaan memperoleh pendapatan dari setiap paket terpasang, sementara penambahan komponen dan penataan ruang berikutnya membuka pembelian lanjutan.”

This is a fictional craft example. It shows a purchasable promise and a repeat occasion, without claiming actual demand.

Weak: “Target pasar kami adalah Gen Z dan seluruh masyarakat Indonesia.”

Stronger: “Penawaran awal ditujukan kepada penghuni kos yang perlu menata meja belajar tanpa membeli furnitur besar. Ukuran modul mengikuti ruang di sekitar meja, sedangkan pemasangan pada akhir pekan mengurangi gangguan terhadap kegiatan kuliah. Pengelola kos menjadi jalur demonstrasi karena masalah ruang terlihat langsung di tempat penggunaan.”

This is an adopted segment/channel design. Local counts, access agreements and measured conversion need their own support. Do not repeatedly prefix design choices with direncanakan, diusulkan or diharapkan.

## Confident design and accurate history

Use “Pelanggan memilih…”, “Perusahaan memperoleh pendapatan melalui…”, “Mitra produksi menangani…”, “Tim memeriksa…”, and “Pesanan diteruskan…”. A designed role can be affirmative without implying a signed contract or completed event.

| State | Accurate wording | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Adopted operation | “Mitra konveksi memotong dan menjahit menurut spesifikasi.” | Accepted design; no signed partner implied |
| Prospective partner | “Penjajakan pemasok memprioritaskan usaha yang mampu…” | Required capability and selection rationale |
| Achievement | “Sebanyak 24 pesanan selesai pada September…” | Actual dated fulfillment records |
| Forecast | “Proyeksi tahun pertama menghasilkan…” | Reconstructable assumptions and formulas |
| Research | “Pada 32 responden yang direkrut melalui…” | Actual instrument, recruitment and responses |

Weak: “Meskipun masih sebatas prototipe hipotetis, ide ini berpotensi menjanjikan dan membutuhkan validasi lebih lanjut.”

Stronger: “Pemasangan dibagi menjadi dua slot harian agar tim dapat menyiapkan komponen dan memeriksa hasil sebelum ruang digunakan. Pertumbuhan pesanan diikuti penambahan tim pemasang ketika slot yang tersedia mampu membayar kapasitas berikutnya.”

Preserve qualifications that change a decision. If a required safety approval controls launch, state that dependency at the relevant step. Do not repeat generic maturity warnings across the proposal or imply approval already exists.

## Financial interpretation

Weak: “Margin 40% dan NPV positif membuktikan bisnis sangat layak.”

Stronger: “Pada harga Rp250.000 dan biaya pemenuhan Rp150.000 per pesanan, kontribusi Rp100.000 membayar koordinasi dan kapasitas tim. Dengan biaya tetap Rp5 juta per bulan, 50 pesanan menutup biaya operasi pada konfigurasi tersebut. Pembelian alat dan jeda pembayaran tetap membutuhkan kas di luar titik impas operasi.”

These teaching numbers are constructed. Contribution divided by price is margin on sales; contribution divided by direct cost is markup. Identify period and included costs. A positive model result is conditional economics, not proven demand. Interpret what the contribution funds, when cash is tight and which lever changes the decision.

## Transitions and rhythm

Weak: “Kami memakai Instagram. Selain itu, kami memiliki SOP. Selain itu, laba meningkat.”

Stronger: “Demonstrasi singkat memperlihatkan ruang sebelum dan sesudah pemasangan. Calon pelanggan kemudian mengirim ukuran meja melalui formulir yang sama, sehingga tim menyiapkan penawaran dan komponen sebelum kunjungan. Persiapan ini membatasi waktu di lokasi dan menjaga jumlah pesanan yang dapat dilayani.”

Carry a subject forward instead of mechanically inserting connectors. Vary sentence length and paragraph openings. Remove generic era digital preambles, feature inventories, ornamental jargon, repeated slogans and final-sounding verdicts in every paragraph. A useful paragraph changes what the reader understands about the business.

## Summary and conclusion

Write the executive summary after the business is coherent: customer, offer, fair differentiation, earning mechanism, fulfillment, decisive economics and growth. It is neither a table of contents nor a national-statistics introduction. The conclusion synthesizes the established commercial case, introduces no new achievements and does not retreat to an indefinite pilot.

## English when requested

Weak: “This revolutionary platform has unlimited potential, although it remains hypothetical and further validation is required.”

Stronger: “Customers book an installation slot and receive a fitted storage set at a fixed package price. The production partner prepares standard parts; the service team measures, installs and checks the finished space. Expansion adds another staffed installation team once existing orders can support its cost.”

Preserve the same distinction between design, forecast and history. An English chat does not switch the default Indonesian proposal language.

## Required review

Read the core with appendices closed. Can a new reader describe one purchase, the reason to choose, paid fulfillment and repeat use? Identify the sentence in each paragraph that advances this case; repair empty paragraphs before adding transitions. Run `python studio.py voice draft.md --output output/voice.json`, then judge every candidate in context. A zero-match result does not certify persuasion or source support. Check achievements against records, forecasts against the model and absolute promises against operating controls. Keep detailed claim states backstage, with material assumptions beside their financial consequences.
