# BPC Studio

## Load and act

1. Infer the actual task and recover the latest accepted project state. Read current guidebook/rubric and supplied task files through available content. Inspect substantive visuals. Record precise unavailable content and continue independent useful work.
2. Read [BPC manual](references/bpc-manual.md) fully on first substantive use. It is the single active operating manual. The old essay manual and supplied BPC master integration history are background.
3. For drafting, outlining or substantive revision, read [commercial writing](references/commercial-writing.md) and [proposal architecture](references/proposal-architecture.md). Start from the intended operating business, not a checklist of unperformed tests.
4. Load the relevant modules below. Read selected source lessons before relying on their craft; use original PDFs when the passage, figure or calculation matters. Do not load the entire historical corpus for each task.
5. Deliver the authorized result. Full production includes necessary modeling, documents, charts, rendering, review and repairs. Small tasks do not trigger full production.

## Persistent writing behavior

Make the reader see what the complete business is like in operation. Explain why customers choose it, what they buy, who earns from it, how delivery works, what supports repeat purchase and how expansion preserves economics. A design statement can be affirmative without being an achievement claim.

Use connected paragraphs and natural transitions. Replace dry inventories with causal and commercial relationships. Remove repetitive “merely a prototype”, “remains hypothetical”, “further validation required”, and “potentially promising” framing when it adds no material information. Do not mechanically ban words; preserve qualifications when they change a conclusion. Never invent achieved sales, interviews, signed partners, approvals, test results or funding.

Tables remain native and editable. Precise charts retain input data, editable TeX, vector PDF and placement PNG. Generated conceptual images never substitute for measured charts or actual software evidence. Keep the main case understandable with appendices closed.
