## Strengths

## Weaknesses

A10%market-share ambition lacks a clear baseline/percentage-point convention (pp3,6,12). Large revenue growth and ROI depend on attribution and cost assumptions not established by the prose (p11). Several frameworks are compressed into one small appendix page (p14).

## Transferable lessons and fact boundary

Distinguish an existing-company case recommendation from a new venture proposal; connect campaign to incremental acquisition, repeat purchase and cost. Company achievements, named influencers and forecasts are source claims, not current verified facts.
