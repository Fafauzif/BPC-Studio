## Reading coverage

All available native/OCR page text was read during this build; extraction alone was not counted as reading. Important actual rendered pages inspected: 14, 38, 42, 45. Other illustrations are not claimed fully transcribed.

## Strengths

Documents production steps and a BOM rather than only attractive packaging (pp26–38). Process/capacity graphic connects batch time and output (p14). Financial appendix retains adverse early cash years (p38).

## Weaknesses

Sales12,250/year (p20) conflict with KPI9,401/month and1,000/month capacity (p45). Markup on cost is called margin. Forecast prices rise while unit costs fall without the supporting operating bridge (p38). Product photographs do not independently establish safety or nutritional performance.

## Transferable lessons and fact boundary

Tie production yield, packaging, labor and demand to one unit. Keep actual sample photos distinct from tests and future sales. Do not reuse recipes, safety claims, respondents, partners or financial returns.
