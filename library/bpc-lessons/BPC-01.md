## Strengths

## Weaknesses

Claims AQI/O2 effects and citywide70%emission reduction without a measured project result (pp10–11,20,22). The funding table and initial NPV outflow need reconciliation, while tiny embedded spreadsheets cannot support precise independent reconstruction.

## Transferable lessons and fact boundary

Connect installation, upkeep, component costs and recurring service. Keep environmental mechanism separate from measured effect. Do not reuse Algrow prices, claimed effects, funding, institutions or research as a new venture fact.
